The "Sniffies Project" is a set of tools that recreate and expand functionality for [sniffies.com](https://sniffies.com). It builds as a Chrome extension, an iOS/desktop/Android userscript, and a bookmarklet.

**Account types:** This tool has only been tested against free and paid Sniffies accounts. It has not been tested against anonymous accounts.

## Features

### Premium Features

Sniffies puts some features behind a paid subscription. "Premium Features" is a group of features that unlock/recreate some of these paid subscription features. If you already have a Sniffies paid subscription, you don't really need me to recreate that paid functionality for you. The panel will detect a paid subscription and tell you that you can turn this set of features off.

{/* TODO GIF: Premium Features section. Record: open the panel, flip the master toggle, then flip each sub-feature switch. Could double as the opener for the three sub-feature GIFs below. */}

**How to use it:** open the "Premium Features" section, turn on the toggle, then turn on whichever sub-features you want.

#### Location spoofing

Override the GPS coordinates Sniffies sees so that you can browse the map from anywhere.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript (iOS Safari, Tampermonkey on desktop or Android). **Not supported on the bookmarklet.**

**How to use it:** switch on "Location spoofing", then click "Travel Mode" on the map. Search for a location and press enter. After the search completes (don't manually move the map), click Sniffies' "Travel Here" button. On reload, the map will be centered on the searched location.

#### Opening profiles outside your free radius

Click on, view, and chat with profiles outside the free geofence.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript, Bookmarklet.

{/* TODO GIF: Profiles outside the radius. Record: pan the map beyond the free radius, click a profile that would normally hit the paywall, and show it open. ~8s. */}

**How to use it:** switch on "Open profiles outside boundary", then click any profile on the map.

#### Hiding sent photos

Hide or unhide photos you've sent. Press on a sent photo until it reveals the options to "Hide Photo" or "Unhide Photo". Or, in the profile menu, click "Hide all Photos" or "Unhide all Photos".

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript, Bookmarklet.

{/* TODO GIF: Hiding sent photos. Record: tap "Hide Photo" on a sent photo, show no paywall and the page reloading with the photo hidden. ~8s. */}

**How to use it:** switch on "Hide sent photos", then use the buttons as usual. The page reloads afterward so the photo shows as hidden.

### Bot reporting & blocking

Report suspected bot accounts (malware-link spammers, fake "OnlyFans" profiles) from their profile screen. Once a report is validated, that account gets filtered out of the map, chat, and live updates for everyone with blocking enabled.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript (iOS Safari, Tampermonkey on desktop or Android).

{/* TODO GIF: Bot reporting. Record: open a bot profile, tap the report button, type a reason, submit; then show the popup counter of blocked bots. ~15s. */}

**How to use it:** open the panel and turn on "Block Bot Accounts". The blocked-account list updates automatically and will filter out any reported bots. If you come across a bot, tap the report button on its profile and submit a report; validated reports get filtered out for everyone with blocking enabled.

### Profile filters

Adds extra filters to Sniffies' filter menu: Gender, Height, Weight and Online After. It also lifts the "Choose up to 3" cap on Sniffies' own Sexuality, Body Type and Position filters (raising it to an arbitrarily high "Choose up to 99").

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension and Userscript for everything; Bookmarklet for the raised selection caps only.
