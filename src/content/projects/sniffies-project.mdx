A set of tools that recreate and expand functionality for [sniffies.com](https://sniffies.com), shipped as a Chrome extension and an iOS/desktop/Android userscript{bookmarkletEnabled && ", plus a fallback bookmarklet"}.

## Features

### Location spoofing

Override the GPS coordinates Sniffies sees, so you can browse the map from anywhere.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript (iOS Safari, Tampermonkey on desktop or Android).{bookmarkletEnabled && " Also possible via the Bookmarklet, though that build is unreliable."}

**How to use it:** open the extension/userscript panel, enable location spoofing, enter coordinates, and save. It can take a moment for Sniffies to pick up the change — reload the page if it doesn't update right away.

### Opening profiles outside your free radius

Click on profiles outside the free geofence to view them without hitting Sniffies' paywall.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension only.

**How to use it:** nothing to turn on — just click any profile on the map and the extension handles the rest.

### Bot reporting & blocking

Flag suspected bot accounts (malware-link spammers, fake "OnlyFans" profiles) from their profile screen. Once a report is validated, that account gets filtered out of the map, chat, and live updates for everyone with blocking enabled.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript (iOS Safari, Tampermonkey on desktop or Android).

**How to use it:** open the panel and turn on "Block Bot Accounts" — the blocked-account list updates automatically, nothing to maintain. If you come across a bot, tap the report button on its profile and submit a report; validated reports get filtered out for everyone with blocking enabled.

### Profile online notifications

_In development — not yet available to install._ Favorite a profile and get a text message when they come online.
