A set of tools that recreate and expand functionality for [sniffies.com](https://sniffies.com), shipped as a Chrome extension and an iOS/desktop/Android userscript, plus a fallback bookmarklet.

## Features

### Premium Features

Sniffies puts some features behind a paid subscription. Premium Features groups the ones the extension can unlock into one section of the panel, with a master toggle and a switch for each sub-feature. If you already have Sniffies Plus, the panel tells you that you don't need them.

{/* TODO GIF: Premium Features section. Record: open the panel, flip the master toggle, then flip each sub-feature switch. Could double as the opener for the three sub-feature GIFs below. */}

**How to use it:** open the "Premium Features" section, turn on the master toggle, then switch on the sub-features you want.

#### Location spoofing

Override the GPS coordinates Sniffies sees, so you can browse the map from anywhere.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript (iOS Safari, Tampermonkey on desktop or Android).

{/* TODO GIF: Location spoofing. Record: open the panel, enable location spoofing, enter coordinates, save, and show the Sniffies map jump to the new place. ~12s. */}

**How to use it:** switch on "Location spoofing", enter coordinates, and save. It can take a moment for Sniffies to pick up the change — reload the page if it doesn't update right away.

#### Opening profiles outside your free radius

Click on profiles outside the free geofence to view them without hitting Sniffies' paywall.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript, Bookmarklet.

{/* TODO GIF: Profiles outside the radius. Record: pan the map beyond the free radius, click a profile that would normally hit the paywall, and show it open. ~8s. */}

**How to use it:** switch on "Open profiles outside boundary", then click any profile on the map.

#### Hiding sent photos

Hide or unhide photos you've sent without hitting Sniffies' paywall. "Hide Photo" / "Unhide Photo" on a sent photo, and "Hide all Photos" / "Unhide all photos" in the profile menu, now perform the action instead of showing the paywall.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript, Bookmarklet.

{/* TODO GIF: Hiding sent photos. Record: tap "Hide Photo" on a sent photo, show no paywall and the page reloading with the photo hidden. ~8s. */}

**How to use it:** switch on "Hide sent photos", then use the buttons as usual. The page reloads afterward so the photo shows as hidden.

### Bot reporting & blocking

Flag suspected bot accounts (malware-link spammers, fake "OnlyFans" profiles) from their profile screen. Once a report is validated, that account gets filtered out of the map, chat, and live updates for everyone with blocking enabled.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension, Userscript (iOS Safari, Tampermonkey on desktop or Android).

{/* TODO GIF: Bot reporting. Record: open a bot profile, tap the report button, type a reason, submit; then show the popup counter of blocked bots. ~15s. */}

**How to use it:** open the panel and turn on "Block Bot Accounts" — the blocked-account list updates automatically, nothing to maintain. If you come across a bot, tap the report button on its profile and submit a report; validated reports get filtered out for everyone with blocking enabled.

### Profile filters

Extra filters in the Cruisers section of Sniffies' Map Layers menu: Gender, Height, Weight and Online After. It also lifts the "Choose up to 3" cap on Sniffies' own Sexuality, Body Type and Position filters.

**Supported on:** Chrome Extension and Userscript for everything; Bookmarklet for the raised selection caps only.

{/* TODO GIF: Profile filters (best candidate for a hero GIF at the top of the page). Record: enable Profile Filters, open Map Layers > Cruisers, set Gender, Height and Online After, tap "Reload to apply", and show the map thinning out. ~20s. */}

**How to use it:** open the panel, turn on "Profile Filters", then open the Cruisers section of the Map Layers menu. Gender, Height, Weight and Online After need a page reload after each change — the menu shows a "Reload to apply" button.

### Profile online notifications

_In development — not yet available to install._ Favorite a profile and get a text message when they come online.
